Getting around fast

Travel shows the whole coast. Drag along it and the marker stays on the shoreline, with a magnifier and a live preview. Fine-tune with the ±100 m to ±10 km buttons, then press Go here.

Monuments lists 43 famous places. Pick one to fly there and circle it.

Share spot copies a link that opens at exactly where you are.

Time, sound and detail

The slider sets the time of day in IST. The sun and moon are where they really are for this date. After dark, the lighthouses sweep their beams.

Sound turns on surf, wind, engines and gulls.

Layers has the detail setting. Choose Balanced or Light if your computer slows down or the browser runs short of memory.

About the data

Terrain and satellite imagery stream from Cesium ion, using your own free token. Buildings come from OpenStreetMap. The coastline route is from Natural Earth.

Monument and lighthouse positions are accurate to within a few hundred metres. Boats, people and waves are illustrations.
